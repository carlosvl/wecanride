We Can Ride

Automated Screening Emails

User Guide for Staff

Overview

Two new automated emails have been set up in Salesforce to streamline communication with new clients during the screening and enrollment process. These emails are sent automatically — no manual action is needed beyond what you already do in the system.

Key Point

These emails are sent only to new clients (New Registration waivers under NewClientPolicies). Volunteers will never receive these emails.

Staff Visibility

Both emails automatically CC clients@wecanride.org, so staff will receive a copy of every email sent to clients.

1

Screening Scheduled

Subject Line Screening Appointment Confirmation
Sent From clients@wecanride.org
Sent To The client's email address (from their Contact record)
CC clients@wecanride.org (staff copy)
Attachments None
Template "Screening Scheduled" (in Salesforce Email Templates)

When does it send?

This email is sent immediately when you enter a Screening Date/Time on a new client's waiver record for the first time.

Step by Step

  1. 1 Open the client's NewClientPolicies waiver record
  2. 2 Fill in the Screening Date/Time field with the scheduled date and time
  3. 3 Save the record
  4. 4 The email is sent automatically to the client with the date and time included in the message

Important Notes

  • The email only sends the first time you enter a date. If you change the date later, it will not send again automatically.
  • The client must have a Contact record with an email address linked through their Client (Rider) record. If no email is on file, the email cannot be sent.

What does the email say?

The email welcomes the client and includes:

  • The scheduled date and time of their screening (pulled automatically from the record)
  • Arrival and parking instructions (4301 County Rd 24, Maple Plain, MN 55359)
  • What to expect during the 45-minute screening visit
  • Information about the screening fee
  • Next steps after the screening
2

Screening Complete

Subject Line Welcome to the Program - Getting Started & Next Steps
Sent From clients@wecanride.org
Sent To The client's email address (from their Contact record)
CC clients@wecanride.org (staff copy)
Attachments 6 PDF documents (see below)
Template "Screening Complete" (in Salesforce Email Templates)

When does it send?

This email is sent automatically after you complete the screening process and click the "Complete Application" button on the waiver record.

Step by Step

  1. 1 Complete the client's screening and fill in all screening-related fields on the waiver
  2. 2 Enter any additional information needed for the application
  3. 3 Click the "Complete Application" button on the waiver record
  4. 4 The email is sent automatically to the client with all 6 documents attached

Attached Documents

The following 6 PDF documents are automatically attached to every "Screening Complete" email:

# Document
1 Client Handbook
2 Client Registration & Policies
3 Payment Policy Notice — Therapeutic Riding & Hippotherapy
4 (Yearly) Session Dates
5 Therapeutic & Hippotherapy Riding Guidelines
6 Guide for Requesting Classes

These documents are pulled from Salesforce Files. If a document needs to be updated, upload the new version to the same Content record in Salesforce — the email will automatically use the latest version.

What does the email say?

The email welcomes the client to the program and includes:

  • Enrollment and deposit information ($150 deposit)
  • Cancellation policy details (30-day notice for full refund)
  • How to request future sessions through the portal
  • How to prepare for their first class (attire, helmet, etc.)
  • Contact information (clients@wecanride.org)

Troubleshooting

The client didn't receive the email

  • Check the client's Contact record — make sure there's a valid email address on the Contact linked through the Client (Rider) record.
  • Check their spam/junk folder — ask the client to look in spam for an email from clients@wecanride.org.
  • For Email 1 — the email only sends the first time a Screening Date/Time is entered. If the field already had a value and was changed, the email won't resend.
  • For Email 2 — make sure you clicked the "Complete Application" button (not just saved the record).

I need to resend an email manually

These automations do not currently support manual resending. If a client needs the email resent, contact your Salesforce administrator for assistance.

I need to update one of the attached documents

Upload the updated document as a new version of the existing file in Salesforce Files. The email will automatically include the latest version going forward. Do not delete and re-upload as a new file — that would break the link.

A volunteer received a client email

This should not happen — the automations only trigger on New Registration waivers under the NewClientPolicies template. If this occurs, please notify your administrator as the record type may have been set incorrectly.

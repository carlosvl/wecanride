## Thank-you list (calendar 2026 through 2026-10-21)

101 rows. The list is longer than 100 because five donors tie at $300, which is rank 97. Everyone at that amount is included.

Five of those rows are organizations with no contact on the gift. Eight people have no phone. Three people have neither phone nor email.

## How a row is chosen

- A client is enrolled on a class with a start date in the window. The contact is the contact on the client record.
- A volunteer is enrolled on a class with a start date in the window. The contact is the contact on the volunteer class, or the contact on the volunteer record when the class row has none.
- A donor is the primary contact on a closed-won donation. If that is blank, the account’s one-to-one contact is used. If neither exists, the row is the organization.
